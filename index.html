The Project

Distributed Flood Detection Network

A network of solar-powered, LoRa-connected sensor nodes deployed in rural or urban areas to measure local water levels and relay readings across a peer-to-peer mesh. A central server aggregates point measurements and cross-references them against a Digital Elevation Model (DEM) to interpolate flood extent and depth in near-real time.

Targeting clogged drainage systems and overland flooding in areas without traditional gauge infrastructure.

LoRa Mesh ESP32 / STM32 Solar Power GIS / DEM Spring 2026
Key Challenges
  • — P2P LoRa mesh routing without centralized gateways
  • — Sustained solar power budget through overcast conditions
  • — Waterproof sensor enclosures with exposed sensing elements
  • — Flood extent interpolation from sparse point measurements
